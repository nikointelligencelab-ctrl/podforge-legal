PodForge Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

PodForge ("we", "us") is a content creation tool that generates videos and publishes them to the user's own social media accounts, including TikTok. This policy explains what information PodForge collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

When you connect your TikTok account to PodForge through TikTok Login Kit, we receive only the data covered by the permissions you approve:

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok so PodForge can upload or publish videos to your account on your behalf (video.upload / video.publish).

We do not collect your TikTok password, your followers, your messages, or any content from other users.

2. How we use information

  • To identify which TikTok account videos are sent to.
  • To upload or publish videos that you created with PodForge and chose to post.
  • To show you the status of your uploads.

We do not use TikTok data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than providing the posting feature.

3. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored locally on the device running PodForge and are not sent to any third-party server other than TikTok's official API. We use reasonable measures to protect this data, but no method of storage is completely secure.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Data is only exchanged with TikTok as required to perform the actions you request.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your tokens only while your account is connected. You can disconnect at any time by:

  • removing PodForge's access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, or
  • contacting us at the email below to request deletion of any stored data.

When access is revoked, stored tokens and profile data are deleted.

6. Children

PodForge is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by TikTok in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, with an updated effective date.

8. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: djnehot@gmail.com
